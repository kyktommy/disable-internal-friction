## Key Adlerian Concepts

- **課題分離 Separation of tasks** — whose problem is it really?
- **目的論 Teleological thinking** — behavior serves a purpose, not just past causes
- **不完美的勇氣 Courage to be imperfect** — striving ≠ perfection
- **社會興趣 Social interest** — contribution as source of meaning
- **生活風格 Lifestyle** — the unconscious map we live by
- **橫向關係 Horizontal relationships** — equality vs. vertical hierarchy
- **共同體感覺 Community feeling** — belonging without approval-seeking
